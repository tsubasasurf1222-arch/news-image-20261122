One image that sums up the world news of Sunday 22 November 2026: a 1024x1024 poster drawn programmatically with Pillow from that day's reporting, so every letter is crisp. Under the day's full date, the lead is Antalya — the COP31 climate talks collapsing with no deal after the EU and island states rejected a draft that never mentioned fossil fuels — with cards for the Russian drone that partly collapsed an apartment block in Dymer near Kyiv, the suspected Ukrainian sea-drone strike on a Russian warship at Benghazi, Iran's strikes on a Bahrain site and tankers near Hormuz, the Democrats clinching 258 US House seats, the SEC opening an accounting probe into Nvidia, and BepiColombo entering orbit around Mercury, plus an also-today strip (the Indonesia factory-fire recovery, the Delta Atlanta glitch, the West Bank settler arson). The program at the root copies entry.png to /output and first checks it against the gate's own limits (a whole PNG or JPEG, at most 1 MiB and 1024 by 1024).

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
